Flying you to the most active storm on Earth right now. Every dot is a real bolt, seconds old — white when it lands, ember-red as it cools.

See the thunder coming

Every strike sends out a ring growing at the real speed of sound. When a ring crosses your house, that's the exact moment you hear the rumble. Turn sound on and the map plays it too — close strikes crack, far ones roll.

No storm near you? Borrow one.

The big glowing bolt is STORM. One press flies you to the most active storm on Earth right now; press again to hop to the next. Somewhere, it is always storming.

On iPhone: warnings and rain, to the minute

The iOS app adds official National Weather Service storm warnings as push notifications — US only, even with the app closed — and a minute-by-minute strip of when rain starts and stops at your house over the next hour.

Made by a dad and a kid ⚡

Downstrike is a father–son project by Leo and Noah Zimmerman, built with help from Claude. It's the lightning map we wanted to watch from our own window — free, no accounts, no ads, and it stays that way. The strikes come from Blitzortung.org, volunteers around the world listening for lightning with antennas in their backyards.
